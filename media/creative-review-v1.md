## Three weakest moments

### 1. 00:00–00:34.7 — the film spends its opening on premises

At3seconds and9seconds, the viewer sees effectively the same wide hero page. The first frame is black from the fade. The product introduction arrives around13seconds. The substantive combination-count claim arrives around26seconds. The edit manifest confirms an unchanged underlying screenshot from18.6–34.7: **16.1seconds** under a statistics caption.

The audience has to accept several abstract promises before seeing a consequential decision. The attractive park is framed as one column of a dense webpage rather than as the film's hero object. The white page, footer strip and small figures compete for attention. “4,096 combinations” is technically meaningful, but the native evidence sits at the bottom edge of the interface and is much less conspicuous than the editorial restatement.

**Rebuild:** begin with a consequential contradiction in the first three seconds: €30k, four commitments, zero feasible plans. Show the actual no-plan state in a tight, readable crop. Then reveal the real €37k repair and one feasible plan briefly, withholding the explanation rather than withholding the product. Establish the illustrative-site disclosure immediately. Introduce the name after the viewer has a reason to care. Cut at least20seconds from this setup.

### 2. 00:43.7–01:00.6 — the causal story quietly changes underneath the explanation

The previous action protects inclusive play. At43.7seconds, that protected scenario is still visible. The next sequence invokes a preset that **resets that protection**, then narrates a budget reduction. The lower third does disclose the reset, but its supporting sentence is small and competes with the budget headline. At47seconds, the viewer is asked to absorb an empty plan, three repair buttons, changed context and later a textual conflict certificate.

This weakens the most important argument: exactly what was held fixed, what changed, and why the result changed. The mechanics are truthful, but the cut asks too much inferential work of a judge.

**Rebuild:** use one clean experiment for the main film: original minima at€55k → budget only at€30k → conflict →€37k repair. Put the protected-play branch after this main proof, or omit it from the hero cut. If both must remain, show a clearly legible “Back to the original commitments” reset before changing the budget. For the conflict, crop to the four actual requirements and reveal them in a deliberate reading order. Do not make the audience read the footer while the novelty happens above it.

### 3. 01:11.4–01:40 — no visible delivery payoff

The narration announces export and a machine-checkable certificate. At71.4 and76seconds, the frame is the hero/top of the page, with an export button; it does not show the exported document or independent verification. This is an assertion where a judge needs proof. From81.445–92.6, the edit holds the footer screenshot for approximately11.15seconds. The final stretch returns to essentially the opening composition and a general slogan.

The useful result is a decision package people can inspect, yet the film ends before revealing that package. The long future-pilot/limitations passage is honest, but placing almost all of it at the climax drains the payoff.

**Rebuild:** record an actual export, open the real JSON/report, expose the chosen interventions, assumptions and verification result, and run the real certificate checker if available. Then return to the **repaired** park beside a concise decision receipt: original minimums retained, €37k, one feasible combination. Keep limitations legible in a compact caption and one spoken sentence. End on the concrete result, with a clear live-demo/source action, not another website scroll.

**Art direction:** keep the warm paper, forest green and terracotta. Give the park a dominant composition with a deliberate camera path or original explanatory geometry; mark conceptual visuals as such. Use fewer, larger captions. Move numbers to the point of action rather than keeping a dense108-pixel strip throughout. Transition by following a concrete element—budget figure, access loop, selected intervention—rather than generic wipes. New render geometry must explain the exact same scenario rather than inventing a more attractive outcome.

**Sound direction, proposed rather than heard:** one concise voice track; a restrained rising texture through the infeasibility sequence; a deliberate release at the feasible repair; a small, specific action sound for exported evidence. No constant whooshes. First decide the edit and word count, then align the sound to the actual changes. Have a human or audio-capable reviewer check intelligibility and emotional pacing at normal speed before release.

## What must change before claiming a major improvement

1. Concrete product consequence within the first eight seconds.
2. One unbroken main experiment with the fixed assumptions obvious.
3. Actual exported evidence shown, not merely announced.
4. Remove the16.1-second setup hold and the11.15-second footer hold; meaningful reveal time replaces dead time.
5. Make the crucial values readable at an ordinary embedded viewing size, not only when pausing the1080p master.
